   capture request IDs complement the reservation. Errors prohibit blind retry.

   response must agree before receipt creation. Receipts omit payer data and
   distinguish fixture evidence. A digest is not provider attestation.

### Additional payment-instruction gap

Order reconciliation ignored `purchase_units[].payment_instruction` while
checking the merchant and gross amount. PayPal's [create-order reference](https://developer.paypal.com/api/orders/v2/orders-create)
documents platform fees, delayed disbursement, special pricing and FX
instructions as payment-processing inputs. Those are outside this example's
signed intent. Capture evidence could also contain platform fees or delayed
disbursement that was discarded during minimization.

Fourteen new adversarial fixture cases failed before the fix: four instruction
variants at CREATED, APPROVED and COMPLETED, plus two completed-capture variants.
The fix rejects these observed instructions before capture when visible there,
and rejects success receipts if they appear only after capture. The adapter
still cannot undo a capture or make a provider GET/capture atomic.

A fifteenth test confirms ordinary PayPal processing fees and default INSTANT
disbursement remain permitted. The buyer's approved gross amount is not a
guarantee about the merchant's net proceeds. These tests establish a missing
source-level rejection, not that PayPal was exploited or accepts a particular
external mutation. Full provider-response compatibility still needs a genuine run.
